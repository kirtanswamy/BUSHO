# BUSHO — AI Tools Usage Disclosure Worksheet

The HSC submission asks teams to disclose AI-tool use, including how tools were used and an estimate of the proportion of final work involving AI assistance or generation.

**Complete this worksheet using your team's actual process before submitting. Do not copy an estimate unless all team members agree it is accurate.**

## Disclosure to finalize

- **Tools used:** [List the AI tools actually used by the team.]
- **Tasks assisted:** [For example: brainstorming, code assistance, debugging, writing, visual design, documentation, or presentation refinement—include only those that actually occurred.]
- **Extent of assistance:** [Provide the team's honest estimate of the proportion of the final work that involved AI assistance or generation, and explain how the estimate was made.]
- **Human responsibility:** Team members reviewed and remain responsible for the submitted code, claims, design, and documentation.
- **Validation:** [State which parts were tested by the team and which remain untested.]
- **AI in the deployed prototype:** The current BUSHO demo uses mock/rule-based extraction and does not perform live AI inference.

## Suggested disclosure format

> AI tools were used for: [actual tasks]. The team estimates that approximately [team-agreed estimate]% of the final work involved AI assistance or generation, based on [brief explanation]. Team members reviewed the resulting material and are responsible for the submitted work. The deployed prototype's extraction is mock/rule-based; it does not use live AI inference. Testing completed: [actual tests]. Known limitations: synthetic inventory, browser-local storage, no production authentication/backend, and no live health-system integration.

Replace every bracketed field with verified information. If a tool was not used for a listed task, remove it. Do not imply that AI generated or validated functionality that was not tested.
