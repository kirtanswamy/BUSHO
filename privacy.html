Privacy Notice

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 17 November 2026

Prototype notice. BUSHO is a development-stage workflow prototype, not an approved government health service. This notice describes the current code and known service providers. It is not a statement that BUSHO is ready to process real patient or sensitive health information. Do not enter real patient-identifying information or real clinical records.

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

For privacy questions or requests concerning BUSHO, contact the project contact at kirtankswamy@gmail.com. Please do not send patient records, passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information by email. This is a project contact, not a formally appointed Data Protection Officer or government grievance authority.

2. Information the prototype may handle

  • Account and profile information: email address, display name, preferred language, role, account status and facility or administrative assignments.
  • Workflow information: worker-entered report text, structured draft fields, review decisions, stock/replenishment workflow records, timestamps and audit events.
  • Technical information: information necessary for authentication, security, service operation and troubleshooting, which may be processed by the hosting, authentication or database providers.
  • Voice input: where supported and enabled by the user, the browser's speech-recognition implementation may process microphone audio. Processing may occur through the browser provider. BUSHO does not operate its own audio-transcription server or intentionally retain raw audio recordings.

Do not submit patient names, phone numbers, Aadhaar or other government identifiers, precise addresses, photographs, diagnoses, prescriptions, medical records, or any other identifiable or sensitive personal information. Demonstration content must be synthetic and clearly identified as such.

3. Why information is used

Within the prototype, information is used to authenticate users, apply role and facility access rules, create and review workflow records, maintain operational traceability, recover drafts where implemented, and troubleshoot or secure the service. Information is not used by BUSHO to diagnose, triage, recommend treatment, make procurement decisions or determine eligibility for public services.

4. Legal basis and consent

The lawful basis for any processing in a future pilot must be determined by the organisation that decides the purposes and means of processing, with appropriate legal review. The administrator provisioning form now asks the administrator to attest that this notice was shared with the invitee before an invitation is sent. The attestation records the administrator's confirmation and timestamp in the provisioning operation; it is not a worker acknowledgement and is not evidence of the worker's consent. BUSHO does not currently implement a worker-side notice-acknowledgement or consent-management workflow. Optional voice input should remain optional and must not be a condition of using text reporting. This notice does not claim that all current processing is consent-based.

5. Service providers and disclosures

The current application is hosted through Vercel and uses Supabase for authentication and database services. The public landing page loads web fonts from Google Fonts. Browser-native speech recognition may involve the browser or its speech-service provider. These providers may process technical or content data as necessary to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not claim that these providers are the only possible recipients in every browser or deployment configuration. The project does not intentionally sell personal information or use advertising trackers as a product feature.

Before a real pilot, the project owner must verify the exact provider configuration, data-processing terms, hosting region, subprocessors, cross-border transfers, retention settings and any additional scripts or SDKs present in the deployed build.

6. Sharing

Role-authorised users may see workflow information needed for their assigned responsibilities. Access should be limited by role and facility. Information may also be processed by the infrastructure providers listed above, or disclosed where required by applicable law. BUSHO does not claim that a government department, health authority or facility has access to this prototype unless a specific authorised integration is established.

7. Retention and deletion

A complete, approved retention schedule and self-service deletion workflow have not been established for all account, workflow, backup and audit data. Some records may remain in the connected development database until removed by an authorised project administrator or according to provider backup cycles. Do not use the prototype for real operational or patient records. For a request relating to prototype account or data, email kirtankswamy@gmail.com with the account email and a description of the request; do not include sensitive records. We will assess the request and explain what can be actioned. Identity verification may be needed before account-related action.

8. Security

The application includes authentication and role-based access controls in its development implementation. This does not guarantee that the service is secure or that every deployment has been independently assessed. Do not reuse passwords, share accounts or enter real sensitive information. Vulnerabilities should be reported privately through GitHub Security Advisories.

9. Your requests and grievances

You may contact us to ask about information associated with your prototype account, request correction or deletion where technically and legally possible, or raise a privacy concern. We will review requests in light of applicable law, security, audit obligations and technical constraints. This project contact should not be represented as a statutory grievance officer. If BUSHO is later operated by an organisation for a real pilot, that organisation must publish its legal identity, designated contact, grievance process and applicable rights before collecting real personal data.

10. Children

The prototype is not designed for children to create accounts or submit personal information. Do not submit information about a child or patient.

11. Changes

This notice may be updated when the application or its providers change. The version and review date above will be updated when material changes are made. Users should review the notice before using a changed service.

12. Important limitation

This notice is an operational transparency document for a prototype, not legal advice and not a substitute for a pilot-specific privacy impact assessment, data-processing agreements, security review or legal approval under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules as they come into force.
