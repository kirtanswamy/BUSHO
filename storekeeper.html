Your role in the supply chain

What happens after a supervisor verifies a field report.

  1. Receive the handoffA supervisor verifies a report, creates a replenishment recommendation for the same facility, approves it and records the external indent submission reference. Only then does it enter your queue.
  2. Record allocation and dispatchEnter references from the actual authorised supply process. BUSHO records evidence; it does not allocate or dispatch medicines itself.
  3. Confirm physical receiptRecord the goods-receipt reference and cumulative quantity physically received. Partial receipts remain open until completed.
  4. Reconcile evidenceReview observations and receipt movements separately. BUSHO does not calculate a verified stock balance without a complete opening and outbound ledger.

Report verification is not a medicine order. A verified report becomes a supply handoff only when the supervisor creates and advances a linked replenishment recommendation.

ACCOUNT & GUIDANCE

Storekeeper responsibilities

What you can do

  • View replenishment requests released to your assigned facility.
  • Record actual allocation and dispatch references.
  • Record cumulative physical receipt quantities and goods receipt references.
  • Review SHC-to-PHC stockout handover cases assigned to your PHC.
  • Review dated inventory observations and receipt movements.

What BUSHO does not do

  • It does not create official government indents or purchase orders.
  • It does not allocate, transport or dispatch medicine stock.
  • It does not certify a live stock balance from incomplete receipt data.

If a verified report does not have a corresponding replenishment request, ask the supervisor to create and advance a supply recommendation. Report review and stock fulfilment are separate workflows.

BUSHO records external references supplied by authorised staff. It does not place orders or connect to DPDMIS automatically. Stock movements shown here are receipt records, not independently verified physical stock balances.
