FACILITY SUPPLY OPERATIONS

Operational overview

Receive approved replenishment handoffs, record external supply actions and reconcile receipt evidence.

Your role in the supply chain

What happens after a supervisor verifies a field report.

  1. 1. Check PHC Stock AvailabilityCheck official PHC registers/DPDMIS records and physically verify usable stock for an accepted SHC stockout request. This dated observation is evidence, not an authoritative stock balance.
  2. 2. Branch 1 — PHC Stock Available (Handover)Record the official PHC-to-SHC issue in the prescribed register/system, then log the handover quantity, actor, timestamp, and optional reference in BUSHO for SHC collection.
  3. 3. Branch 2 — PHC Stock Unavailable (External Indent)Mark PHC unable to fulfil. Once the authorised PHC user submits the consolidated facility indent in DPDMIS/government portal, manually record the external reference and track upstream receipt at PHC before onward SHC handover.
  4. 4. Close After SHC Physical ReceiptThe SHC worker physically counts and confirms receipt (or records any shortage/discrepancy). Cases close only after last-mile SHC receipt evidence is recorded.

BUSHO tracks coordination handovers and external references only; official paper registers and DPDMIS/CGMSC remain the authoritative systems of record.

ACCOUNT & GUIDANCE

Storekeeper responsibilities

What you can do

  • View replenishment requests released to your assigned facility.
  • Record actual allocation and dispatch references.
  • Record cumulative physical receipt quantities and goods receipt references.
  • Review SHC-to-PHC stockout handover cases assigned to your PHC.
  • Review dated inventory observations and receipt movements.

What BUSHO does not do

  • It does not create official government indents or purchase orders.
  • It does not allocate, transport or dispatch medicine stock.
  • It does not certify an authoritative stock balance from incomplete receipt data.

If a verified report does not have a corresponding replenishment request, ask the supervisor to create and advance a supply recommendation. Report review and stock fulfilment are separate workflows.

BUSHO records external references supplied by authorised staff. It does not place orders or connect to DPDMIS automatically. Stock movements shown here are receipt records, not independently verified physical stock balances.
