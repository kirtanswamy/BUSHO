BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

A few important details.

Is BUSHO a live government health platform?

No. The current website is a synthetic data prototype. It is not connected to government health systems or facility databases.

Does BUSHO diagnose diseases?

No. It structures reported observations for human review. It does not make clinical decisions.

Can workers report by voice?

The prototype supports browser native speech input where available. Recognition depends on browser and language support.

What data should I enter?

Use synthetic examples only. Do not enter real patient names, phone numbers, identifiers or sensitive health information.

Does BUSHO store voice recordings?

BUSHO does not store audio. Browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser provider, depending on its service.

Can I use real patient information in the demo?

No. The prototype is for synthetic examples only and has no production security or clinical data infrastructure.
