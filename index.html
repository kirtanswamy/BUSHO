WORKFLOW FREEZE V1.0

One exception. Two controlled fulfilment branches.

Every step is recorded as evidence, with human decisions and physical receipt kept explicit.

STAGE ASHC worker

Physically observes low stock or stockout and submits an exception to the verified parent PHC.

→
STAGE BPHC reviewer · MOIC / designated supervisor

Reviews the request: accepts for PHC action, requests clarification, or marks unable to fulfil.

BRANCH 1

PHC stock available

  1. PHC storekeeper checks official stock and records a dated availability observation.
  2. PHC-to-SHC handover is recorded with quantity and source reference.
  3. SHC worker collects and confirms physical receipt or discrepancy.
  4. Case closed after receipt evidence.
BRANCH 2

PHC stock unavailable

  1. PHC storekeeper records unavailable status from a physical/official check.
  2. Authorised PHC user submits the consolidated indent in the official government portal and manually records its external reference.
  3. Upstream supply is received at PHC and receipt evidence is recorded.
  4. PHC-to-SHC handover is recorded; SHC worker confirms physical receipt or discrepancy.
  5. Case closed after receipt evidence.

Explicit exclusions: no direct CGMSC warehouse-to-SHC delivery, no routine BMO/CMHO approval hops, no automated DPDMIS API submission, and no Jan Aushadhi Kendra/PMBI inventory.

COEXISTENCE & SYSTEM BOUNDARY

What remains official. What BUSHO coordinates.

Official records and authorityBUSHO coordination and evidence
Paper registers

Remain the official local record and manual continuity route.

DPDMIS / DVDMS

Remain the official supply portal and indent record.

External reference tracking

Stores a manually entered reference only after authorised submission occurs outside BUSHO.

CGMSC and procurement authority

Remain responsible for official upstream procurement and supply.

PHC-to-SHC handover evidence

Clinical portals and clinical authority

Remain separate. BUSHO does not diagnose or direct treatment.

Operational exception trail

Maintains facility-scoped workflow events and review decisions, without patient identifiers.

Evidence, not inventory truth

Every quantity is tied to an observation, receipt, source and timestamp. BUSHO never labels it a live or official stock balance.

Human decisions stay human

PHC acceptance, external portal submission, allocation and physical handover remain actions of authorised personnel.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers about BUSHO.

Is BUSHO an official government portal?

No. BUSHO is an assistive prototype and does not claim government ownership or official integration.

Does BUSHO replace DPDMIS, CGMSC or paper registers?

No. Official registers, supply portals, procurement authorities and clinical systems remain authoritative.

Does BUSHO calculate a live stock balance?

No. It records dated physical observations and receipt evidence. It does not certify an authoritative inventory balance.

Does BUSHO submit an indent automatically?

No. An authorised PHC user submits the consolidated indent through the official government portal and manually records its reference in BUSHO.

Can patient details be entered?

No. Stockout cases must not contain patient names, phone numbers, ABHA IDs, diagnoses or other patient-identifiable information.

What if connectivity is unavailable?

Use the official paper register and authorised manual handoff process. Reconcile the operational record in BUSHO when connectivity returns.
