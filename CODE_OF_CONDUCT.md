# Code of Conduct

BUSHO is built for a social cause, and the community around it should reflect that.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and kind. Assume good intent and disagree with ideas, not people.
- Welcome contributors of every background, language and experience level.
- Give feedback that is specific, constructive and actionable.
- Respect the people the project is meant to serve: never share real patient or personal data in issues, pull requests or screenshots.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information, or any conduct that would make others feel unsafe.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation when this code is violated. To report a concern, contact the maintainer, @kirtanswamy, through GitHub.
