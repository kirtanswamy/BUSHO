Accessibility information

Last reviewed: 17 November 2026

Ongoing work. BUSHO is working toward an accessible experience. This page is not a claim of WCAG conformance or independent accessibility certification. A full manual audit across supported browsers, assistive technologies and languages has not yet been completed.

What we aim to support

  • Keyboard access to navigation, forms and primary actions.
  • Visible focus indicators and a skip link to the main content.
  • Programmatic labels and instructions for form controls.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and accessible names for controls.
  • Readable contrast, responsive layouts and zoom/reflow.
  • Clear validation messages and status updates that assistive technologies can announce.
  • Text entry as an alternative to optional voice input.

Known limitations

Accessibility can vary by page, language, browser and assistive technology. Some workflow tables, dynamic states, icons, translated strings and third-party browser features may not yet provide an equivalent accessible experience. Automated checks alone do not establish conformance. We are continuing keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, zoom and mobile testing.

Report an accessibility barrier

If you encounter a barrier, email kirtankswamy@gmail.com. Please include the page or workflow, the action you were trying to complete, your browser/device and (if comfortable) the assistive technology used. Do not include passwords, patient information or other sensitive data. We will review feedback and consider an accessible alternative.

Target

Our design target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA, with relevant Government of India Guidelines for Indian Government Websites and Apps (GIGW) considered as a reference. This is a target, not a certification or claim of current compliance.
